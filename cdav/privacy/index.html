LuneOS C+DAV

Privacy Policy

What the LuneOS C+DAV connector does with your account, contacts and calendars.

This policy covers LuneOS C+DAV, the LuneOS account connector that synchronises contacts and calendars with Google and with other CardDAV and CalDAV servers. It is free software maintained by the webOS Ports community (“we”). The source code is public.

The short version

  • LuneOS C+DAV runs on your device. It connects directly from your device to Google, or to the server you choose.
  • We run no server for it, and we do not receive, store or sell your account data, contacts or calendars.
  • It contains no advertising or analytics.

Google data the app accesses

When you add a Google account, Google asks you to approve these permissions:

PermissionWhy it is needed
auth/calendarRead your calendars and events, and send changes you make on the device back to Google.
auth/carddavReach your contacts through Google’s CardDAV service.
auth/contactsRead your contacts, and send changes you make on the device back to Google.
auth/userinfo.emailGet your email address, which identifies the account on the device.
auth/userinfo.profileRequested with sign-in. The app does not keep your profile details as part of the account.

How your data is used

Your data is used only to sign in to your account and to keep the contacts and calendars on your device in sync with your account. This includes sending changes you make on the device back to the account.

LuneOS C+DAV’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Where your data is stored

Everything the app stores stays on your device:

  • Sign-in data, such as access and refresh tokens or a server password, is kept by the system key manager.
  • Account settings, contacts and calendar events are kept in the device database, where other LuneOS apps such as Contacts and Calendar can use them.
  • Contact photos are kept in device storage.
  • Diagnostic logs are written to the device’s system log and to a log file in device storage. They can contain account names, server addresses and details of synchronised contacts and events. Passwords, sign-in tokens and the raw data exchanged with servers are kept out of them. The app does not send logs anywhere. Take care if you copy or share them, for example in a bug report.

Sharing

We do not receive your data, so we have none to share. The app sends data only to Google or to the CardDAV or CalDAV server of the account you add. That provider’s own privacy policy applies to the data it holds.

Keeping and deleting data

Data stays on your device until you remove it. Removing the account in the LuneOS Accounts app deletes its stored sign-in data and settings, and removes its synchronised contacts and calendars from the device. Removing an account does not delete anything from Google or your server.

Contact photos and diagnostic logs may stay in device storage after the account is removed. Erasing the device removes them.

You can also withdraw the app’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account connections page.

Security

Connections to Google use HTTPS. For other servers, the connection uses the address you or your provider’s settings supply, so use an https:// address. Anyone with access to your unlocked device or its storage may be able to reach the data on it.

Children

The app is not directed at children. It may be used only with an account you are allowed to use under your provider’s rules.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project’s issue tracker. Issues are public, so please do not include personal data or logs that contain it.
